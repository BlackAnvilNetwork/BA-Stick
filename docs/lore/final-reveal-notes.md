## Constraints from the bible (do not break these)
- **Dark fantasy only.** The New Kings usurped Anvilhold and erased the makers' history and names by edict, fire, chisel and fear. The land, the stones, the songs and the people remember. The reveal is about grief, faith, craft, betrayal, memory and defiance.
- **Rule of evidence:** struck stone is always true, ink can lie, and witnesses are true but incomplete. Every fragment is one of those three kinds, and the reveal lands on **struck stone**.
- Gold is the currency. Guilds appear only as a light epilogue hint.
- Warp circles are physical but unwired. Only a Time Mage wakes them, one region at a time in campaign order, and the Seven-Stone Ring's waking *is* the finale.
- Places: regions Pale Hollow, Ashlands, Slagpits, Bellmarsh, Throne Approach. Cities the Shard Dwellings (gate of Mayflower), Obsidia, the Great Filter, Belltower Keep, the Citadel of the Seven Seats, with the Anvil Hall at its heart.
- Finale game hook: clearing the Citadel ends a run, and afterwards field enemies grow stronger to match the player.

## Candidate A (canon): "Struck once, true for all": the Seventh Seat was never a throne
**What the player learns.** Under the Citadel, six of the Seven-Stone Ring's stones carry runes re-cut into the Kings' House marks. The seventh is plain and white like House Unnamed's arms. Its original rune is the **hammer laid crosswise**, the open strike scraped off every defaced Anvil crest since Crestcut Knoll. The Seventh Seat stands empty because the seventh Scrivener, Hesk Tallowmere, refused the crown and became the first Unwritten. The seat was never a throne: it belonged to the Hammer, which belongs to no one hand.

**The finale.** Mayflower's makers climb the King's Stair with the Carried Marks and their grandparents' hammers and prove each re-cut stone against its true mark. A Time Mage sings the six original runes back into the stones. The player strikes the Anvil, and it rings for the first time in three hundred years. Across the Reach every chiselled-off name sounds once in its stone; the erased dead hear themselves called and rest. The Anvil's Sparks flood the night sky. The overcut motto *"Struck once, by the Crown"* cracks back to the smith's oath: ***"Struck once, true for all"***, honest work by your own hand, and now a true name given back. The Correction, the stolen Mayflower child, gets his mark back and remembers who he was.

**Where.** Throne Approach → the Citadel of the Seven Seats → the Anvil Hall, at the Seven-Stone Ring on the Anvil Neck.

**Setup beats (campaign order).**
- **Pale Hollow**
  - First hum at the Pale Dust Crystal near the Shard Crypt.
  - On the Defaced Stele, the player notices *something scraped off below the anvil*. It's the hammer; don't name it yet.
  - The Terrace Keeper's tallies count in sevens.
  - The Shard Wights give up the first true rune once they are laid to rest.
  - An Unwritten chalk circle shows **seven** marks, one rubbed out last.
- **Ashlands**
  - Glass-locked tablets: the Burning order "signed by seven hands", one signature struck through. Ink can lie, so it's a hint, not proof.
  - At the Mirror Ring, a reflection shows the original arms with the hammer intact.
- **Slagpits**
  - The Inspector's Book has a "waste" grading signed by six Houses; the seventh column is blank, a refusal.
  - The Reverse Grate turns up a proving-tag for a Scrivener "graded out of the Book".
- **Bellmarsh**
  - The bell-founders' recipe: forge-stone, 7 parts.
  - At the Silent Bell, an Echo Wraith repeats a sentence about "the one who kept the hammer". A witness: true but incomplete.
  - Undertoll was where Hesk's people lived. The Kings drowned it.
- **Throne Approach**
  - Re-headed Statues: seven bodies, six crowned heads, one bare neck.
  - The Scriptorium of Corrections' great books have a seventh row blank in both *was* and *shall be*.
  - The Last Unwritten recites the motto, then knocks (three short, one long).
  - Then the ring.

**After.** A name restored is not a war won. The Seneschal's court, the Scriveners and the cult's remnants push back; Mayflower's makers rekindle the Cold Forges and guard the Ring. Post-finale enemy scaling reads as "the Kings push back; the Ring must be held."
